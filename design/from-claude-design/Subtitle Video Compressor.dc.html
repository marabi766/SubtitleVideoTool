3 · Reading the link
Pasting a link starts a 6–8 second probe. Everything below the link — the video summary, the quality list, the subtitle line — is one block that has three conditions: empty, filled, or failed. The link field, the folder and the access cards never change, so a failed probe is fixed and re-run without retyping anything.
